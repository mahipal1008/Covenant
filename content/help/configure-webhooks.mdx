# Configure outbound webhooks

Covenant ships scan, finding, and contract events to any HTTPS
endpoint you register. Every delivery is HMAC-SHA256 signed with the
secret you provide at subscription time.

## Recommended setup

- One subscription per consumer system (Slack, PagerDuty, custom
  pipeline). This keeps retry budgets isolated.
- Store the signing secret in a secret manager and rotate quarterly.
- Verify the signature **before** parsing the body.

## Replay protection

Each delivery includes a UUID and timestamp. Reject deliveries older
than five minutes and dedupe on the UUID for at least an hour.
