# Manage billing and trials

Plans are billed per active repository per month. Seats and add-ons
(compliance evidence, dedicated regions) are line items.

## Trial mechanics

- 14 days, no credit card required.
- A repository scanned during the trial counts as active for the
  next billing cycle only.
- Trials extend automatically by 7 days if you start a SOC 2 evidence
  pull during the final 48 hours.

## Failed payments

Three retry attempts over seven days, then the workspace moves to a
read-only state. A fourth attempt is not made — clear the payment
method to resume.
