# Letter of Intent — Covenant Design Partner Program

> This is a non-binding letter of intent intended solely to express
> the parties' good-faith interest in entering into a paid commercial
> relationship. It does not create an enforceable obligation to
> purchase the Service. Sections 4 (Confidentiality) and 6
> (Governing Law) are binding.

**Date:** ____________________

**Customer:** ____________________________________________
**Address:** ____________________________________________
**Authorized signer:** ___________________________________

**Vendor:** Covenant, Inc. ("Covenant")

## 1. Subject

Customer intends to enter into a paid subscription to the Covenant
platform on the **Scale** tier (or a tier of equivalent or greater
value) for an initial term of twelve (12) months, beginning on the
later of (a) Covenant's public launch date and (b) the conclusion of
the Design Partner Period defined in Section 3.

## 2. Pricing

Customer will receive the Design Partner Discount: **six (6) months
free** following Covenant's public launch, then **fifty percent (50%)
off** the then-current Scale list price for the remainder of the
initial twelve-month term. After the initial term, standard list
pricing applies unless renegotiated in writing.

## 3. Design Partner Period

Beginning on the date of this letter and ending no later than ninety
(90) days after Covenant's public launch, Customer agrees to:

- Use Covenant on at least one production repository in test or
  read-only mode.
- Provide weekly feedback by call or asynchronous message.
- Allow Covenant to reference Customer's name and logo on its
  website and in marketing materials, subject to Customer's prior
  written approval of the exact copy and placement.

## 4. Confidentiality (Binding)

Each party shall protect the other's non-public information disclosed
during the Design Partner Period using the same standard of care it
applies to its own confidential information, and in no event less
than reasonable care. This obligation survives termination of this
letter for a period of three (3) years.

## 5. No Exclusivity

Nothing in this letter restricts either party from working with any
third party, including competitors of the other party.

## 6. Governing Law (Binding)

This letter is governed by the laws of the State of Delaware, without
regard to its conflict-of-laws principles.

## 7. Definitive Agreement

The relationship contemplated by this letter will be documented in a
mutually executed master subscription agreement and order form. In
the event of a conflict between this letter and a definitive
agreement, the definitive agreement controls.
